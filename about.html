I am a master's student at the University of Chicago and a Data Visualization Fellow at the Center for Digital Scholarship. My work sits at the intersection of geospatial computing, environmental science, and scientific visualization — building tools that make complex environmental data legible and explorable.

At the UChicago Library, I design and deliver geocomputation workshops for the university community and build open-source geospatial tools, including the Chicago Environmental Atlas — an interactive time-series map of 150 years of the city's ecological history.

I am graduating in June 2026 and available for positions in geospatial data science, environmental informatics, and data visualization starting in Fall 2026.
