Introduction to GIS

Fundamentals of geographic information systems — spatial data models, coordinate reference systems, geoprocessing, and cartographic design.

Course Overview

Introduction to GIS established the conceptual and practical foundations for spatial data work. The course covered both desktop GIS environments (ArcGIS Pro, QGIS) and the theoretical underpinnings of spatial data representation and analysis.

Key Topics

Spatial Data Models

Vector data models (point, line, polygon) and raster data models. Understanding when each is appropriate, how they are stored and queried, and how to convert between them.

Coordinate Reference Systems

Geographic vs. projected coordinate systems, datums, and projections. Practical skills in identifying, transforming, and choosing appropriate CRS for different analysis contexts.

Geoprocessing

Core spatial operations: clip, intersect, union, buffer, dissolve, and spatial join. Workflow automation using ModelBuilder (ArcGIS) and processing scripts.

Cartographic Design

Principles of map design — color theory, classification schemes, symbolization, and layout. Producing clear, purposeful cartographic products for specific audiences.

Spatial SQL

Introduction to spatial queries with PostGIS/SpatiaLite — geometric functions, spatial predicates (contains, intersects, within), and joining spatial to attribute data.
