Data Visualization Fellowship

Three-quarter fellowship designing and delivering geocomputation, GIS, and data visualization workshops for the University of Chicago community.

Period Autumn 2025 – Spring 2026 Quarters 3 workshops delivered Host UChicago Library, CDS

Overview

As a Data Visualization Fellow at the University of Chicago's Center for Digital Scholarship, I design and deliver hands-on technical workshops introducing researchers, students, and library staff to geospatial tools and methods. The fellowship spans three academic quarters, with each quarter featuring a distinct workshop topic and audience.

In parallel with teaching, I built the Chicago Environmental Atlas — a flagship interactive map for the CDS — and contributed to the library's geodata infrastructure through the GIS Assistant curation pipeline.

Workshops

Spring 2026 — Geocomputation with Python

Hands-on workshop covering spatial analysis with Python, targeting researchers already comfortable with Python basics. Topics included vector data manipulation with GeoPandas, raster processing with Rasterio, Landsat land surface temperature extraction, and working with CTA/Census geodata for Chicago.

GeoPandas Rasterio Landsat LST CTA data Census geometries Jupyter

Winter 2026 — ArcGIS Online & Survey123

Workshop introducing the UChicago ArcGIS Online environment and Survey123 for field data collection. Participants learned to publish web maps, configure smart forms for field surveys, and integrate collected data back into a web GIS workflow.

ArcGIS Online Survey123 Web maps Field data collection

Autumn 2025 — Introduction to ArcGIS Online

Introductory workshop for community members new to GIS. Covered the ArcGIS Online interface, layer management, symbology, and basic spatial analysis using real Chicago datasets including CTA rail stations, community areas, libraries, and ACS demographic data. Cook County 2020 aerial imagery was used as a basemap context.

ArcGIS Online CTA data Community areas ACS demographics Aerial imagery
